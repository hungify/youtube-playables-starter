# Store listing

Submit these through the YouTube Playables Developer Portal. They are not part of the game bundle.

- **Title:** Zombie Rush (no YouTube branding or logos, per the design requirements)
- **Short description:** Drive, drift and ram waves of zombies in a small arena. Blow up barrels, upgrade your car and survive the boss.
- **Controls:** Touch / mouse: hold and drag, the car steers towards your finger. Keyboard: arrows or WASD (Down / S reverses).
- **Tags:** arcade, driving, action, survival
- **Content notes:** Cartoon zombies, no blood or gore (impacts burst into green slime).
- **Thumbnails:** `store/thumbnails/` holds gameplay captures at 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16. Regenerate them with
  `bun run dev` in one terminal and `bun scripts/capture-thumbnails.ts` in another. Check the Developer
  Portal for the exact pixel sizes it asks for and resize if needed; these are captured from live gameplay.
